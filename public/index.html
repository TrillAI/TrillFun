How TrillFun works

Capture even when muted

System and tab capture read the audio before the volume stage of the player, so a muted player, a muted tab, or a muted system still produces audio for transcription. Nothing needs to be audible.

Keeps transcribing when the page is closed

Stream URL sources are pulled by the server itself. Once tuned in, they keep transcribing 24/7, survive page reloads, and resume automatically after a server restart. Browser-based sources (system/tab/mic) can only capture while a tab is open — that is a browser security rule, not a TrillFun limit — but the stream is remembered and restarts the moment any TrillFun page reconnects.

Radio chatter and 10-codes

The radio preset band-limits to 300–3200 Hz, removes squelch hiss, and compresses clipped PTT audio. Every 10-code is canonicalised (“ten fifty four” → 10-54) and annotated with its meaning. Call signs and the phonetic alphabet are normalised too.

Phone calls

The phone preset is tuned for 300–3400 Hz, hiss and codec artifacts. Speakers are split into A/B turns by pitch; radio traffic is labelled by call sign.

History

Every segment is written to a local database as soon as it is transcribed, with timestamp, speaker, codes, confidence and the audio. Search across all streams, export as text, SRT, VTT, CSV or JSON.

Privacy

Speech recognition runs locally in this app. No audio or transcript leaves the machine.
